## Evidence requirements per photo

For every proposed image, record the exact facility match, source URL, image URL, author, license/permission, attribution requirements, whether modification and redistribution are allowed, and evidence date. Official website visibility alone does **not** grant permission to copy. Wikimedia Commons images require file-level license and author verification. A Google Places photo is not a freely reusable downloaded asset.

Do not use search thumbnails, unlicensed social photos, or generic photos of a different business as a substitute.
